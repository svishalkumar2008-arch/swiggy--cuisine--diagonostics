# Swiggy Cuisine Performance Diagnostic

## Tableau Dashboard Link
https://public.tableau.com/views/Swiggycuisineperformancediagonostic/Sheet1

## Executive Summary & Data Story
During H1 2026, North Indian, South Indian, and Italian cuisines successfully cleared their monthly revenue targets (Above Target). Conversely, Chinese, Fast Food, and Desserts experienced critical shortfalls (Below Target - Critical).

### Actionable Recommendations:
* **Catalog Expansion:** Focus menu expansion and onboarding efforts on the Chinese category, as high average order values suggest unsatisfied customer demand constrained by limited vendor supply.
* **Vendor Operations Review:** Conduct operational health checks on low-tier Fast Food outlets suffering high cancellation rates.
